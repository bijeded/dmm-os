# Imported history is exempt from the lifecycle guards

The folder scan writes Proyecto and Cotización status directly: a folder under `Archivo/` or the external HDD, or an accepted quote with no folder, becomes a *completado* Proyecto, and a quote tied to a folder becomes *aceptada*. It does not go through `completarProyecto` (only completed once fully paid) or `aceptarCotizacion` (creates Ingresos and estimated Costos). This is deliberate. The history predates the app and many old jobs will never have a matching CFDI, so holding them *en curso* until paid would make dead projects and old Contactos look active. Generating Ingresos on imported acceptance would double-count against the Facturas run, which is the real record of invoiced income; uninvoiced Ingresos (no IVA) are entered by hand. We rejected routing the importer through one lifecycle module: with a single importer and a sanctioned exception, it moves code without concentrating it. The guards govern transitions made in the app; `escaneo.test.ts` pins the exception.
